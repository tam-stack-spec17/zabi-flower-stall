Our Story

Led by floral designer Syed Zabi, Zabi Flower Decoration is built on a profound passion for artistry and design. With a keen eye for elegance and a commitment to sourcing only the finest, freshest blooms, our team works intimately with every client to bring their unique vision to life. From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, we believe every special moment deserves to be framed in beauty.
