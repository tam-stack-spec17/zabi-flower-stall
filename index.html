Our Story

Led by floral designer Syed Zabi, Zabi Flower Decoration is built on a profound passion for artistry and design. We specialize in transforming venues into breathtaking spaces through bespoke wedding and event decorations. With a keen eye for elegance and a commitment to sourcing only the finest, freshest blooms, our team works intimately with every client to bring their unique vision to life.

Our Services

Wedding & Pre-Wedding Ceremonies

Exquisite floral designs tailored for Haldi, Nikah, Valima, Shukrana, Receptions, and full traditional Wedding ceremonies.

Venue & Stage Transformations

Grand stage decorations and breathtaking entrance setups customized to perfectly match your specific theme and venue.

Personal Floral Accessories

Custom-crafted bridal florals, groom's floral accessories, and elegant, fresh garlands designed for your special day.

Special Occasions & Parties

Beautiful fresh flower arrangements and venue styling for all kinds of parties, functions, and everyday celebrations.
