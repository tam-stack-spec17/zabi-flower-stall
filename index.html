Our Story

Led by floral designer Syed Zabi, Zabi Flower Decoration is built on a profound passion for artistry and design. We specialize in transforming venues into breathtaking spaces through bespoke wedding and event decorations. With a keen eye for elegance and a commitment to sourcing only the finest, freshest blooms, our team works intimately with every client to bring their unique vision to life. From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, we believe every special moment deserves to be framed in beauty.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to? ▼
We currently deliver throughout Bengaluru and surrounding local areas. For large event decorations or weddings, we can travel further upon special request.
Do you take custom orders for weddings? ▼
Absolutely! Bespoke wedding and event decorations are our specialty. Please tap the WhatsApp button to schedule a consultation with Syed Zabi.
How far in advance should I place an order? ▼
For daily bouquets, 24 hours notice is preferred, though we accept same-day orders based on availability. For large event decorations, please contact us at least 2-3 weeks in advance.
